# Fresh canonical-Ethereum wstETH Axis 5 authority walk

Please perform a fresh, manual, observation-only authority walk for canonical wstETH on Ethereum
and update the canonical Ethereum evidence in `topology/assets/wsteth.yaml`.

The current file is not acceptable as dashboard evidence. It was generated by the iced v2.0
reader, carries the 36.5%-trust warning, and its Ethereum walk stops at `accessors-refused` on the
stETH Aragon application and kernel. Do not mechanically migrate those rows or interpret
`timelock: unresolved` as no delay.

## Scope

Canonical Ethereum only for this phase:

- wstETH `0x7f39c581f595b53c5cb19bd0b3f8da6c935e2ca0`;
- its fixed stETH dependency `0xae7ab96520de3a18e5e111b5eaab095312d7fe84`;
- every live authority path capable of changing code, accounting, mint/burn or holder balances,
  oracle/report acceptance, deposits/staking-module routing, or withdrawal/redemption behavior
  that materially affects canonical wstETH holders.

Start at the wrapper and trace upstream only where the power can affect the stETH represented by
canonical wstETH. Distinguish the wrapper's own control surface from inherited stETH/Lido control.
Record each materially distinct power as its own layer/path rather than collapsing all Lido DAO
authority into one generic controller.

Base, Monad, CCIP/token-pool contracts, cross-domain executors and other bridged deployments are
out of scope. Preserve their existing evidence if the shared asset file requires it, but do not
refresh it, use it in the canonical headline, or let a bridge authority qualify an Ethereum result.

## Required observations

For every in-scope path, resolve and evidence:

- target contract, implementation/proxy mechanism, and whether code is replaceable;
- exact callable power and its present reach;
- direct controller, role administrator, and every intermediate authority hop;
- terminal authority model and address;
- for a Safe, live `getThreshold()` and `getOwners()` results—never infer an m-of-n threshold from
  labels or key counts;
- execution delay, its enforceable floor, bypass routes, proposer/executor/canceller relationships,
  and any distinct reaction window;
- whether the path is active, renounced, absent, or still unresolved;
- a positive control for every negative finding;
- fixed observation block and UTC observation time.

Use the current per-path `actionable-path/1` fields defined by backing-monitor
`specs/axis5-contract-admin-spec.md`: `control_state`, derived `actionable`, `execution_delay`,
`reaction_window`, `reach`, `reach_bound`, `value_at_risk_scope`, `changeability`, `terminal_kind`,
`terminal_address`, `holder_kind`, `independent_canceller`, and `delay_floor`. If the evidence cannot
establish a field, publish `unresolved`; do not infer it to complete the schema.

At minimum, explicitly answer these canonical questions:

1. Does wstETH itself have any upgrade, pause, freeze, mint, confiscation, parameter, or rescue
   authority, or is its deployed behavior fixed apart from the upstream stETH state it reads?
2. What live path can replace or modify the stETH implementation, and what is the shortest
   enforceable execution delay on that path?
3. Which authorities can accept or alter stETH accounting/oracle reports, and what limits or
   sanity checks bound their effect?
4. Which authorities can change staking-router/module allocation or deposit behavior in a way that
   affects backing or availability?
5. Which authorities can change, pause, gate, or upgrade the withdrawal queue/redemption path?
6. Are any observed controls bounded rather than core/full? State the precise bound.

Do not assign an Axis 5 score, assess signer independence, or make the combined smart-contract/admin
judgment. security_analyst owns observations; RiskAnalyst owns interpretation and scoring.
