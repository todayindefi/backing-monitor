What this number is
Ratio basis: Backing / supply INCLUDING the reserve fund (coverage_ratio_with_reserve_fund x 100). The conservative coverage excluding the reserve fund is in backing.secondary and sits lower; the two diverge precisely when the fund is absorbing something. ⚠️ USDe is delta-neutral, not overcollateralised: par is the design target, so the generic [130,110,100,90] rates its intended state 'Watch'. Cutoffs are anchored on this analyzer's own COVERAGE_WARN (1.0) and COVERAGE_CRIT (0.98).
Surplus basis: Implied by the rated ratio: total_backing - total_backing/(collateral_ratio/100), i.e. the surplus INCLUDING the reserve fund. ⚠️ NOT total_backing - total_supply, which excludes the fund and is published as surplus_deficit_ex_reserve_fund.
