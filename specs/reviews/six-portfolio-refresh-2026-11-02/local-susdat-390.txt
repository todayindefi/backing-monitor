What this ratio covers
Ratio basis: NAV per share vs $1-par issuance (1 USDat/share). ⚠️ ATTESTED, NOT MEASURED: 98.6% of the reserve is an OFF-CHAIN STRC claim marked by oracle (breakdown tags it oracle_unverified); only 1.4% is on-chain USDat this repo can verify. Surplus basis: totalAssets minus shares — NAV accrued over $1-par issuance, which sUSDat holders own. ⚠️ NOT a junior-capital question: there is no tranche below this taking first loss, so nothing inside the surplus can be redeemed by someone else. The exposure that dominates it is the MARK, not the composition — ~97% of the reserve is an off-chain STRC claim priced by oracle and tagged oracle_unverified in the breakdown, so the surplus is only as good as that price and any multiple against it inherits that dependency.

NAV is not monotonic here, and a decline does not imply a loss event. ~99% of the reserve is an off-chain STRC claim carried at an oracle mark, so NAV is marked to that price between cycles and falls whenever the mark falls. Over the 714 transitions plotted above, 35 are declines (median -11.9 bps, largest -90.8 bps) while NAV is +0.94% net across the window. Vesting sets the upward drift; the mark sets the noise around it — read a decline by size and persistence, not by direction, because a single hourly tick is ordinary mark movement. Vesting intentionally delays yield landing, so a fresh sUSDat position accrues with a lag at first. Implied APY is computed from the NAV slope over the trailing window; the 11% headline target is a Saturn-disclosed design number, not a guarantee. sUSDat NAV is denominated in USDat (the vault's underlying), not USD. USDat's own peg deviation stacks on top, so $-equivalent NAV ≈ 4626 NAV × current USDat peg.
