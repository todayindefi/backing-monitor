Coverage basis — claims versus reserves
Surplus basis: Minting-source collateral value minus crvUSD supply. ⚠️ THIS IS NOT A CUSHION OWNED BY crvUSD HOLDERS. The overcollateralization is BORROWERS' EQUITY: a borrower can repay and withdraw their excess collateral at will, so it is capital inside the surplus that can walk out — the same shape as yzUSD's junior tranche, arrived at by a different mechanism. What protects holders is the LIQUIDATION machinery (soft liquidation progressively converts collateral as prices fall), not ownership of the excess. So a multiple against this figure answers 'how many times the collateral currently posted', never 'how many times the loss-absorbing capital'.
