Score rationale
What this ratio covers
Supply and backing are both read on Ethereum. The rated ratio is an Ethereum statement. Global supply and the chains it spans are in asset_specific.multi_chain; the BSC leg exposes no backing accessor, so its backing is UNREAD, not absent.
Ethereum bridges by LockRelease and BSC by BurnMint, so Ethereum's totalSupply already contains the bridged portion and summing chains would double-count. See asset_specific.multi_chain for the locked balance and the per-chain figures that ARE measured.
Ratio basis: Ethereum reserve-token holdings / supply, with PYUSDx marked at par. This verifies token-unit coverage, not MoonPay custody or ultimate dollar reserves. totalAssets() counts alternative assets only; primary PYUSDx is accounted separately, so zero is valid for an all-PYUSDx reserve.
Surplus basis: Reserve total minus USDat supply — the net buffer, which is ~0 by design on a 1:1 mint. ⚠️ Whether a subordinated layer sits inside it is NOT ESTABLISHED: USDat itself has no tranche, but its backing moved to PYUSDx and this feed does not measure that structure's capital stack. There is also little to hide in a buffer this size — the exposure here is the MARK and the mint authority, not surplus composition.
