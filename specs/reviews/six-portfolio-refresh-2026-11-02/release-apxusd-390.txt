Tracking (notable accumulation since attestation)
Of Apyx's total reserves, approximately 25% is directly readable on-chain via a single balanceOf call on the STRCx contract at the Apyx Safe. The remaining approximately 75% (brokerage STRC, cash & equivalents and the other reserve lines) is verifiable only through the Accountable TEE-attested proof-of-solvency feed and monthly Wolf & Company CPA examinations.
What the on-chain read establishes, and what it does not. A single balanceOf call on the STRCx contract at the Apyx Safe returns 696,800.76 tokens, and that read is genuine and independent — it is the balance, from the chain, with no issuer in the path. What it does not establish is a dollar figure. Converting that count into the $71.8M (~25% of total reserves) shown above passes through two issuer inputs and one stale one, and none of the three is verified by the on-chain read: (1) balanceOf returns shares, not dollars; (2) those shares are already scaled by Backed's rebasing multiplier() — currently 1.09795 — which is an issuer-set input, not a deterministic accrual, and Backed attests reserves at the xStocks-family level rather than per token, so STRCx's own 1:1 backing is not independently checkable at a moment in time; (3) the per-token price used is $103.0454, derived from the April 2026 Wolf attestation, while the live multiplier-adjusted market mark is $99.14 — the applied price is 3.9% higher, worth about $2.7M of the figure above. The Safe's balance has grown ~39% since that attestation, so the anchor prices a materially different position than the one held today. The honest claim is therefore narrower than “verified reserves”: the token count is independently verifiable on-chain; its value per unit is set by the issuer and marked at an anchor from the April 2026 Wolf attestation. Both can be true while the backing is real — this is a statement about what a third party can check without trusting Backed, not a statement that the assets are absent.
