# Selected component issuer evidence

The shared issuer feed remains `issuer-project-evidence/1`. Its additive `component_profiles` takes precedence over legacy project profiles for the exact migrated asset. `reference_components` contains explicitly selected components with no report/view mapping; it does not create aliases or dashboards.

Producer: `tools/build_issuer_project_evidence.py` plus `tools/issuer_component_projection.py`, through the existing sync. DeFiAnalyst's bounded public-source contract is accepted only at the exact SHA256 in `config/issuer_project_publication.json`. New or changed source bytes require renewed publication review. Missing, changed or invalid source produces explicit unavailable component placeholders; it cannot fall back to private parent profiles, family terms, underlying rights or stale records from that source.

The accepted contract selects24 facts across10 components:9 mapped profiles and the separate STRCon reference. There is no database dump or automatic public visibility change. Original entities/roles, status, basis, source dates, observation dates, checked dates, direct URLs and coverage states are retained. Unknown dates stay null. `not_exported` is not searched-not-found, a finding that rights are absent, or permission to borrow another component's facts.

The UI keeps authored scores/interpretation and presents selected sourced fields separately. Raw MSTR/STRC views receive a component card through their existing bespoke renderer. STRCx uses the corrected normal RiskAnalyst export: Backed Assets (JE) Limited is issuer, Backed Finance AG tokenizer, Security Agent Services AG security agent. Brokers/custodians are disclosed roles, not proven current allocations. Strategy's STRC preferred rights are not MSTR common rights or wrapper-holder rights.

Financial-statement audits, reserve-asset examinations and smart-contract audits remain separate. Apyx's March asset examination retains its observation/signature dates and does not become current token liabilities or a solvency opinion. Existing Apyx receipt measurement and release code are unchanged. Published documentary governance/control design and legal effectiveness do not establish deployed authority, payment receipts, pass-through, current outstanding or successful redemption.

The October29 certificate's stated November1 effectiveness/November3 first new regular payment are legal-document observations, not receipt evidence or a new dividend-burden calculation. No daily/replaced semi-monthly double count or mechanical repurchase subtraction is introduced.

Pending PT-apxUSD/PT-apyUSD ownership choices are not activated. STRCon has no invented riskAnalyst slug. PT-srUSDat's selected fixed-block read advertises both srUSDat and sUSDat outputs; it is not measured output quantity or guaranteed USDat cash. These references remain separate from token-level rights and actual route execution. srUSDat/PT/STRCon have no newly created dashboard pages.

Changes to source facts do not rescore assets or advance full-verification/score-decision dates. Unavailable legal fields and unresolved custody, enforcement, assurance, payment, PT-quantity and Monad-conversion gaps remain explicit.
